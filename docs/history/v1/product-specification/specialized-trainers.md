## Role and access

In explicitly selected specialist practice, coaches are specialized trainers whose professions shape teaching sequence, activities, follow-ups, feedback priorities and next practice. In the CEFR course, the reviewed curriculum owns those choices; the selected coach presents/supports the same lesson without changing its training requirements. This supersedes the earlier tone/examples/optional-focus-only model. All seven roles are unlocked without gold: Elephant Teacher, Owl Grammarian, Bear Librarian, Fox Journalist, Tiger Business Professional, Parrot Speaker and Creative Writer. Creative Writer's animal, name, artwork and voice remain undecided; do not invent an approved character or reuse an unrelated asset as acceptance evidence.

Specialization is an authored pedagogical contract, not a claim that a character is a credentialed human professional or that seven AI agents/models are required. All trainers share reviewed learning objectives, valid language standards and learner-controlled progress. Personality and background selection remain independent. Subject expertise never permits fabricated facts, mandatory accent imitation or unsupported assessment.

## Trainer methods, activities and criteria

Each trainer has a stable role ID. Authors create versioned child objectives and reviewed activity/rubric packs at the appropriate level; the same role can teach simple A1 language or more demanding advanced work. The following learning criteria apply to a bounded objective, not the trainer's entire profession. Assess unaided responses to fresh tasks after practice; completion and successful performance remain separate.

| Role | Training method / session sequence | Characteristic activities and feedback focus | Initial learning acceptance for a reviewed task |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| TR-TEACH · Elephant Teacher | Explain → demonstrate → guided practice → gradually remove support → independent application | English Foundations; worked examples; sentence construction; brief comprehension checks; focused refresher. Feedback identifies the next prerequisite or step, using plain language. | Learner produces at least 4/5 fresh target responses unaided, then completes the linked communicative task under its reviewed rubric without scaffolds. Knowing a grammar label alone cannot pass application. |
| TR-GRAM · Owl Grammarian | Diagnose a recurring target difficulty → contrast examples → construct/correct/transform → use in a new message | Agreement, verb forms, sentence roles, phrases/clauses; minimal contrasts; meaning-preserving transformations. Feedback explains the specific pattern rather than correcting everything. | At least 4/5 fresh productive responses meet the selected target/meaning; appropriate target use in two natural opportunities in an unfamiliar linked task. Apply EF criteria; recognition alone is insufficient. |
| TR-LIB · Bear Librarian | Understand in context → distinguish meanings → retrieve → use → revisit later | Short reading/listening passages; word-sense comparisons; collocations; paraphrase; optional review enrollment. Feedback separates misunderstanding, failed retrieval and grammar. | At least 2/3 first-response comprehension answers per assessed modality; at least 4/5 selected senses recalled and used appropriately in original phrases/sentences, with delayed checks reported separately. Recognition cannot pass productive recall. |
| TR-JOUR · Fox Journalist | Listen/read → interview → ask a relevant follow-up → clarify → summarize accurately | Source-based questions; specificity challenges; fact/opinion/uncertainty distinctions; accurate paraphrase. Feedback prioritizes relevance, missing detail and fidelity to the source. | Learner asks two relevant questions including one response-dependent follow-up, and summarizes three predetermined key points without material contradiction or invented detail. Oral listening evidence requires an audio source; text supports reading-based evidence only. |
| TR-BIZ · Tiger Business Professional | Establish audience/purpose → simulate exchange → organize answer → handle follow-up → revise | Introductions, updates, requests, meetings, interviews and constructive disagreement; optional PREP/BLUF/STAR support. Feedback addresses clarity, relevant evidence, register and next action. | Listener/reviewer can identify the purpose, two task-relevant facts/reasons and a suitable next step; learner answers one realistic follow-up while preserving supplied/actual facts. Reviewed simple A1 equivalents are allowed; framework acronym use is never required. |
| TR-SPEAK · Parrot Speaker | Listen to a reviewed reference → notice target → record/replay → targeted retry → apply in a fresh utterance | Word/sentence stress, phrase grouping, supported sound targets and intelligibility; comfortable pacing. Feedback prioritizes audible communication impact and only supported observations. | On two fresh utterances designed for the selected target, a qualified listener can recover the intended meaning and judges the taught target sufficiently realized using reviewed audio anchors. No automatic pass from transcript correctness, faster delivery or accent imitation. Unsupported audio assessment stays not assessed; self-review remains useful. |
| TR-WRITE · Creative Writer | Imagine/observe → plan → draft → targeted revision → tell/retell → vary perspective/audience | Short stories, descriptions, dialogue, word-choice challenges, scene rewriting and spoken retelling. Feedback preserves the learner's intended meaning/voice while developing coherence, detail and expression. | Learner produces a coherent reviewed three-part sequence (setup, event and outcome), uses two relevant concrete details and answers one comprehension/follow-up question consistently. A new prompt checks transfer. Written composition, revision and unaided spoken retelling are scored separately; imaginative fiction is labeled as fiction. |

Counts above are initial bounded-task defaults, not universal rules for every answer. Qualified review must set level-appropriate length/complexity, natural opportunities, valid alternatives and anchored success examples before publication. A1 may use three simple story sentences or a basic work request; advanced tasks need richer criteria. Optional simplification/assistance stays available and is recorded as assisted, never silently passed as independent performance. Style preferences such as verbosity or vividness cannot become grammar errors.

## Common session and evidence contract

Each published trainer pack defines role/method version, primary learning objective, linked CEFR/EF objective and descriptor mapping, expected activity sequence, support/fading rules, task/answer/rubric versions, source/reference assets, rights/editorial state, capability requirements and reviewed offline fallback. A changed mascot or greeting without the role-specific activity/feedback sequence fails trainer differentiation.

Common experience: state a practical goal → brief instruction/example → role-specific attempt → grounded feedback/self-review → optional retry → later return/new situation. Normally provide up to two prioritized actionable improvements; valid work may warrant zero corrections. Offer an explanation and retry without gold or paid AI success. For fictional/personal tasks, preserve the selected fact/fiction mode; never turn a character's story into an employment accomplishment or learner history fact.

Core completion is attempting the pack's explicitly listed core activities and reviewing corrections/self-review, not meeting a score. Standalone packs must list exact core activities before publication. Embedded trainers retain the CEFR lesson's completion rule and cannot silently add required sessions. Quiet/text alternatives support completion with speech/listening unassessed as appropriate; audio and transcript/feedback saving remain independently opt-in. No trainer implies a validated pronunciation capability or clinical service.

Record role/method/objective/task/rubric versions, primary versus optional specialty objective, modality, assistance, target opportunities, assessment purpose and source attempt. Keep common grammar/vocabulary evidence linked across trainers, with specialist outcomes separate. A trainer switch never resets history or pays twice for the same event. Freeze the substantive method/task/support/rubric during an assessment. A presentation-only coach switch at a safe boundary preserves the test; an actual method/task/support change starts a separate assisted/practice attempt. Do not compare differently scaffolded or specialized tasks as a reliable improvement trend without reviewed comparability. No seven unrelated English-proficiency scores or automatic level promotion.

For retained specialty skill, repeat a comparable fresh role-specific assessment around day 7 (±2 days) and day 28 (±3 days) after initial teaching, with actual exposure/delay recorded. Report each criterion separately; missing evidence is unavailable. These windows may reuse the Introductions pilot schedule but do not replace its vocabulary or communication checks. Independent unfamiliar-task success, delayed success and within-learner improvement are distinct. A single-owner result cannot establish general efficacy.

## Use within the app

- **Learn:** the CEFR lesson retains its reviewed sequence, teaching/core activities, difficulty and assessment whichever coach presents it. Offer optional related specialist practice as an explicit separate context, preserving return position. Coach preference never substitutes story/interview drills for required course content.
- **Speech Lab:** choose an available trainer session by skill—interviewing, grammar application, workplace communication, delivery or storytelling. Show task goal, support and capability availability before starting. Librarian/Writer may also offer quiet/text sessions within Learn.
- **Today:** suggest a trainer with an evidence-based reason, such as difficulty retrieving selected words → Librarian or recurring agreement errors → Grammarian. Weak terminology alone does not demote communicative ability. Insufficient evidence falls back to stated goals/due work, not diagnosis. Learners can choose another available session.
- **Vocabulary / Progress:** optional shared word enrollment and reviewed recall rules remain intact; display objective evidence, trainer context and support used. Participation rewards and learner-reported confidence stay separate from specialist performance.

All seven roles are free to select in delivered content. An internal build may show a role with no published pack as planned/unavailable, without a gold lock or invented activity. Public-release acceptance requires reviewed available packs and the stated trainer criteria for all seven roles; exact level-by-role volume belongs to SC-T006/SC-T044 review and cannot be implied from seven selection cards. Optional voice preview/TTS depends on supported capabilities, with text fallback and no autoplay in quiet mode.

## Introductions and staged delivery

First demonstrate a genuinely distinct Teacher → guided introduction, Grammarian → agreement/construction practice and Journalist → relevant follow-up session around the shared INTRO-01 topic. Keep this as a choice of practice paths, not a forced three-coach circuit. SC-T018 authors the three bounded packs, SC-T020/SC-T021 connect their entry/progress, and SC-T043 tests them. The independent three-fact introduction/follow-up, activity minimums and optional assessment remain unchanged. A Journalist question-writing exercise is additional specialty practice, not a new mandatory INTRO-01 core item.

Later packs connect Librarian to contextual vocabulary/comprehension; Business Professional to audience-aware workplace tasks; Speaker to reviewed reference/audio practice; Writer to creative writing and spoken storytelling. No role is dropped from adopted scope by staging. Use [SC-T044](../development-plan/tickets/SC-T044.md) for decomposition/coverage and [trainer acceptance checks](../testing/specialized-trainer-acceptance.md) for content, behavior and learner proof. Artwork or schema validation alone cannot close specialized teaching quality.

## CEFR invariance and independent conversation — owner clarification

[Course and practice boundaries](learning-path-and-practice.md) supersede any earlier wording implying seven different CEFR lesson methods. The profession-specific table applies to specialist packs outside required course training. CEFR objectives, progression, difficulty, core instruction/activities, completion and assessment are fixed across all seven presenting coaches; optional help is recorded as assistance. Shared evidence does not permit unattempted course completion or automatic level advancement.

AI conversation and interview practice are separate Speech Lab capabilities, not synonyms for specialist monologue feedback. Conversation includes back-and-forth turns; interviewing includes response-dependent follow-ups and afterward review. Recorded-feedback delivery remains first and live streaming/turn-taking/interruption remains a later investigation.
