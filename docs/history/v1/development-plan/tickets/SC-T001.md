## Scope and decisions

- M2 internal proof: explicitly record an introduction, replay/re-record, finish offline self-review, or explicitly request transcription → learner confirmation → afterward text coaching; offer retry and independently opt-in audio/text history. No key/network is required for local practice.
- M3 first topic uses the already adopted single coach-independent INTRO-01 required lesson and minimum pack. Short resumable teaching/activity segments may be arranged during SC-T018/SC-T020 without changing sequence, counts or completion. Optional specialist packs remain separate practice; no additional required CEFR variants or topic volume are introduced.
- Owner confirmed on 2026-10-21: internal introduction proof range 1–120 seconds, subject to feasibility; optional three-second countdown off initially, learner can enable it. General recording maximum is deferred to SC-T002/SC-T003, not inferred from this proof.
- Retain approved defaults: English interface/American English teaching, model answer hidden until requested, explicit online action, original recognition separate from confirmed revisions, up to two grounded feedback actions with valid zero-correction output, and independent opt-in retention.
- Core completion: six comprehension questions, five grammar items, five productive recall cues, correction/model review for misses, and one guided introduction plus self-review. Quiet text route counts toward completion; declined speaking remains unassessed. Baseline, AI, saved payloads and assessment success are optional. Optional concept checks do not add core requirements.
- Preserve reviewed text/audio model, five senses, five grammar items, guided task, independent baseline, two reserved transfer prompts, rubric/anchors, self-review and delayed checks. See INTRO-01 for exact requirements; educator review and the four-week learning gate remain SC-T018/SC-T043, not completed here.
- Exclude from this proof: realtime dialogue, full-course delivery, generated curriculum, precise pronunciation scores and full reward-system delivery. Existing adopted public-release scope is retained; no scope expansion or cancellation.

Remaining decisions are assigned, not silently defaulted: architecture/local capture/storage/OS/signing and proof feasibility SC-T002; provider/models/region/transport/quota SC-T003; temporary cleanup/deletion/evidence SC-T004; offline packaging/versioning SC-T005; publication/reviewer and first-topic mappings SC-T006; initial background, first-flow assets/font rights/native design SC-T007; reviewed lesson segmentation, answer-reveal/repeated-error assistance SC-T018/SC-T020; scheduler SC-T022; economy/day/reset policies SC-T025.
