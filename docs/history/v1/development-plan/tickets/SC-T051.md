## Implemented precedence — 2026-10-22

A saved choice for the local calendar day wins when that destination is still available, and the grounded reason stays on screen. Yesterday's choice does not carry over. A review choice is ignored when nothing is due. Otherwise the newest supported difficulty wins. At the same time, grammar outranks retrieval, then follow-up, then clarity. An undated observation loses to a dated one. With no difficulty, due reviews come first, then the ready or unfinished lesson (Introductions when nothing else is open), then Speech Lab. The suggestion names one action. The other destinations stay available. Follow-up and clarity are accepted only from a reported observation, not from duration or completion.
