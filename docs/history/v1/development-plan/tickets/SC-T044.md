Reuse early Teacher/Grammarian/Journalist evidence; add Librarian, Business Professional, Speaker and Creative Writer methods/activities and level-appropriate continuation. Coordinate workplace coverage with SC-T035 and communication/articulation with SC-T036, without duplicating engines, records or rewards. Creative Writer's animal/name/artwork/voice remain owner design decisions; unresolved visuals must be tracked, not invented. All roles are unlocked; unavailable internal packs are not represented as delivered. No separate agent/model/provider per coach is assumed.

## Required behavior and constraints

Each pack references shared CEFR/EF objectives, reviewed method/task/rubric versions and capability/retention rules. Core instruction and self-review work offline with installed assets. Personalized cloud feedback requires supported capability and explicit requests. Preserve facts versus fiction, valid English varieties, free help/retry, learner choice, frozen assessments and meaningful differentiation beyond tone. No clinical, unsupported acoustic or automatic CEFR claims.

- SC-T040 release audit includes actual pack/content/device/learner evidence; unresolved visual/voice choices have an explicit completion decision, not invented approval.
