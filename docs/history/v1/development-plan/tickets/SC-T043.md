## Scope and decisions

Freeze and educator-review the four-week protocol before baseline; obtain consent; execute the bounded owner pilot and iPhone/iPad functional checks; collect delayed recall, repeated baseline and unfamiliar-task evidence; review retained audio blinded to order and compare AI feedback against human judgment. Record actual exposure, deviations, insufficient evidence and an explicit expansion/rework decision.

Exclusions: public efficacy claims, complete A1 certification, realtime dialogue, stack/provider selection, automatic uploads or mandatory private-payload retention. Qualified review and consent are prerequisites, not presumed available. Existing explicit cloud-request/billing authorization and retention rules apply.

## Required behavior and constraints

All IL checks retain their own status. Completion, learning success, confidence and engagement stay separate. Supported/text-only practice remains accessible without receiving an independent speaking pass. Missing/deleted audio cannot support a blinded acoustic comparison. Numeric thresholds are pilot defaults, reviewed prospectively rather than adjusted to fit observed outcomes.
