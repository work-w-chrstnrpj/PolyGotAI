## Scope and decisions

Generate 68 separate earned/base PNG masters with built-in ImageGen, using one request per asset and the adopted board as a style reference. Use exact AW IDs, subject and circle/shield/hexagon/checkpoint classification. Keep app labels, tier numbers and evidence-state overlays separate from the illustration; only adopted A1–C2 course marks may be intrinsic text. Save under assets/awards/v1/ with per-asset prompts/provenance and a machine-readable manifest.

Exclusions: application code, reward ledger, actual earning events, new catalog rules, Figma writes, provider/account setup, animation implementation, independent bitmap state/tier instances, editable vector/layered masters and native/device acceptance. Raster delivery does not fulfill those later production contracts. Do not claim upscaled PNGs are vector masters or a concept sheet is 68 assets.

## Required behavior and constraints

Use true transparency around isolated artwork, consistent optical sizing/safe margins and readable subject silhouettes. AW-CR-01/02/03 remain unframed checkpoint illustrations. Trainer completion and skill signatures share identity in their adopted different shells. Preserve original reference assets and generated-source files; no overwrites or cropping a contact sheet as a substitute for individual generation.

## Acceptance criteria

- Exactly 68 unique family IDs map to existing nonempty PNG masters; three checkpoint icons have no collectible shell.
- Subjects, family shells and permitted embedded course text match the adopted mapping; inspect all outputs and report/revise failures rather than treating generation as visual PASS.
- Actual image dimensions, color/alpha properties, file integrity and source provenance are verified; no placeholder, blank or duplicate file passes coverage.
- All requested finals reside in the workspace with a browsable labeled gallery and manifest; exact prompts/reference paths and built-in generation method are recorded.
- Device/accessibility/native rendering, state overlays, vectors, animation and reward behavior remain explicitly NOT_RUN/not delivered by this scope.
