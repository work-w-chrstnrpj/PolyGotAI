## Update rules

- Ticket files own their detailed status, readiness, decisions and completion evidence. Update the ticket and this table together when work starts, a dependency changes, a blocker appears or verification finishes.
- Update milestone counts and the review date in the same change. Keep roadmap and parent SC status aligned when their actual outcome changes.
- Use pending, in_progress, awaiting_testing, blocked or done. Awaiting testing means implementation complete with acceptance work remaining; missing implementation/content/decisions stay in_progress. Awaiting dependencies does not itself mean actively blocked work; record the specific blocker when blocked applies.
- Evidence is NOT_RUN until checks occur. Link sanitized results or the ticket completion record; never mark done from elapsed time, drafted documentation or partial checks.
- Later outlines require child tickets. Add each child to this tracker and roadmap, retain its parent link, and revise counts. Ticket counts describe tracking coverage, not weighted product completion.
- Existing SC-003 design work is in_progress in the feature tracker; its new child tickets remain pending until their own scoped work starts.
