# SC-D013 confirmed review fixes — 2026-10-24

Owner authorized fixing the three confirmed issues from Codex's review of Grok's staged delivery. The real Xcode project remains unmodified by this pass; a temporary review-only project registers the staged source/tests for compilation and hosted verification.

## Changes

- Conversation text discard clears turn text, situation, support/focus and self-review text. Interview discard clears answer/follow-up/review/technical text, preparation and supplied facts in all attempts, the draft preparation and user-entered role title. Audio retention remains a separate choice. Domain mutators refuse new preparation, facts, technical notes and self-review after a retention choice is applied. Views clear their draft fields/attachments and reset/disable the self-review form, preventing stale local state from restoring declined text.
- `InterviewRecordingSlot` binds each recorder launch to a new UUID, exact attempt and question. Unrelated, late, blank or repeated attachments are refused. Opening and follow-up recording buttons establish different slots; a successful submission consumes its slot. A changed task drops the old slot; an unsuccessful/no-op task revision preserves the current answer. This replaces the shared recording reference that could attach the opening audio to a later question or attempt.
- `InterviewSession.reviseSupport` closes the active request as cancelled before superseding the attempt. A late result cannot enter the new attempt. No provider request is actually sent by these screens.
