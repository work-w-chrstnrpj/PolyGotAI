## Recorded feedback lifecycle proposal

States: ready → recording → recorded → reviewing locally → submitting → receiving feedback → completed, with recoverable failed/cancelled states. Permission denial, interruption, route changes, network loss and provider failure need distinct outcomes. Preserve the local attempt when online feedback fails. Submission must be an explicit action; retries must not duplicate local attempts or completion rewards; provider charges may recur and require controlled explicit retries. No automatic background upload contract is selected.

Persist optional recordings separately from metadata; make retention/deletion behavior explicit before implementation. Transcription, grammar suggestions and pronunciation assessment are separate capabilities. Show uncertain recognition and unsupported assessment honestly. General-purpose language-model output must not fabricate sound-level scores or official CEFR certification.

## Vocabulary scheduling proposal

Use an Anki-style scheduler. SC-T046 selects FSRS-6.0 from the MIT-licensed swift-fsrs library at commit `d09cfc96c8f4c135ffc1905ac5627672906bf421`, vendored at `application/Vendor/swift-fsrs`. The latest tagged release, v5.0.0, implements FSRS-5 only, so the pin is that commit rather than a floating branch. Desired retention is 0.90, the maximum interval is 36,500 days, interval fuzz is off, and short-term steps are 1 minute then 10 minutes. Hard on a new card is 6 minutes. Again, Hard, Good, and Easy keep their adopted meanings. Intervals adapt to history. Vocabulary → Review options now exposes desired retention (strictly between 0% and 100%) and an optional positive session-size limit. The existing 90% target and unlimited session remain defaults. A higher desired retention generally increases workload. Preferences apply prospectively: no edit or restoration of defaults rewrites a due date or manufactures a rating. An interrupted eligible card comes first before limiting one session; the full due backlog remains visible to Today and is available in later sessions.

Maintain separate recognition and productive-use evidence. Recognition schedules only after an explicit rating of a revealed answer. A hint is stored as assistance and is not unaided recall. Lesson exposure, grammar correction, and productive use do not move the recognition due time. Saving a word to the collection does not put it in the due queue; Add to review keeps the same card and offers it. A revealed card that has not been rated is stored as an open recall and is resumed without a rating. The same source event cannot schedule twice. Identity is the item and sense, so editing the wording keeps the card and a new sense does not inherit it. A card with no enrollment is not offered for review. Store the rating, review time, card kind, and scheduler version. A review time before the card's last review is refused, and an unknown scheduler version is left unchanged. Due times are absolute instants. The library counts elapsed days from UTC midnights. The repository clock does not move backward. AI sentence feedback does not choose the rating.

Sources: [Anki deck options and FSRS](https://docs.ankiweb.net/manual/deck-options#fsrs), [Anki FSRS FAQ](https://faqs.ankiweb.net/frequently-asked-questions-about-fsrs.html), [Anki studying](https://docs.ankiweb.net/studying.html). These are scheduler references. Executable checks are in `application/.build/lesson-review-loop-v2.xcresult`. A device walkthrough of the lesson and recall screens remains open. Parameter fitting remains open.

## Dynamic activity generation policy

Owner-approved direction: combine editorially reviewed curriculum with AI-generated practice variations. Keep objective IDs, CEFR difficulty constraints, target grammar/vocabulary and assessment criteria stable while varying examples, situations and prompts. Generation must not silently expand prerequisites or publish a new curriculum objective. Learners can configure fresh versus familiar practice; exact defaults await design.

Proposed pipeline: select an approved objective/template → submit minimal relevant context to the protected integration → receive structured activity, answer rules and explanation → validate structure, objective fit, difficulty, American English and answer consistency → present/cache an accepted variation. Runtime validation is distinct from qualified editorial review; generated output must not be mislabeled as educator-reviewed. Reject ambiguous, incorrect or unsupported output and use a reviewed fallback. Validation methods, provider, numeric limits and release coverage remain open engineering decisions.

Fresh cloud generation requires connectivity. Offline practice uses reviewed bundled variations or previously generated activities that passed validation and remain compatible with the current objective/version. Generation failure must preserve the current attempt and offer a fallback without mandatory retry. AI generation is not required to open basic lessons.

Vocabulary variations preserve word sense, card identity, retrieval target and review history; changing a sentence must not reset Anki-style scheduling or leak the answer before recall. Keep assessment variants separate from ordinary randomized practice. Baseline tasks remain repeatable; alternative comparison tasks require comparable difficulty and versioned criteria before reliable improvement claims. Freeze the presented activity and its answer/rubric for the lifetime of an attempt.

Generated pronunciation sentences require suitable reference audio and validated assessment for the target; language-model generation alone cannot establish pronunciation scores. Resource-based activities use learner notes or legitimately available, reviewed source material; a YouTube URL alone supplies no video-content access. Minimize private context, constrain requests to learning purposes and validate generated content as untrusted data.
