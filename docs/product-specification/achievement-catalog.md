## What this inventory counts

A **badge** is a collectible representation of an eligible achievement. An **achievement** is its rule/evidence event. A **milestone** marks a defined course/goal threshold and need not have collectible artwork. **XP/gold** are economic entries, not proficiency. The same event may produce several truthful descriptions, but cannot mint duplicate rewards.

This list has **68 award families**: fixed badges, explicitly listed tier families and parameterized lesson/topic/unit/objective families. It is not a claim of exactly 68 finished badge images. Exact lesson/topic/objective/sense-set instances depend on reviewed content and the release completeness matrix. Never invent those counts or activate a family over an empty/partial prerequisite set. This is the adopted v1 family inventory against current scope; later product scope changes require a catalog revision.

Award identifiers use the AW- namespace, distinct from EF curriculum objectives and TR trainer-role IDs. The owner adopted all listed family names, classifications and initial tier values; implementation and visual art remain separate. "Carried forward" identifies the original seven-item subset; "Added in v1" identifies the 61 adopted additions. Neither provenance label means implemented or earned. Do not expose planned conversation/live modes, unpublished levels or unsupported audio assessment as earnable merely because a catalog row exists. Visual design follows catalog/rule review; this document selects no shape, palette, rarity material, tier artwork or animation asset.

## Common eligibility rules

- Participation/completion requires the defined core attempts plus correction/self-review; success scores are not completion gates. Quiet/offline alternatives retain equivalent general participation credit; modality-specific awards must state the actual modality.
- Learning badges require a named versioned objective/task/rubric and qualified review of level, natural target opportunities and exact thresholds before activation. An unseen independent task, assistance/modality and delayed evidence are explicit; missing/declined/deleted evidence is unavailable, not a pass or proof of forgotten skill.
- For this adopted catalog, learning badges apply the source objective’s unfamiliar-task and delayed criteria, normally day 7 ±2/day 28 ±3 from initial teaching. Record actual practice and delay; source-specific criteria take precedence. If the source has no reviewed numeric rubric or delayed-task design, that badge remains blocked pending definition rather than auto-awarded. Initial numbers are operational proposals, not validated CEFR cutoffs.
- A one-time task success may appear as a progress observation before a retained-learning badge is eligible. Course completion, retained ability and improvement are different claims. Baseline-to-comparison improvement additionally requires reviewed comparable evidence; no extra generic “English mastered” badge is defined.
- Fixed one-time badge identity uses family/rule version and learner; parameterized badges add objective/content-set identity; tier awards add threshold. Currency/reward identity remains linked to the original eligible event, not the number of badges. Cross-entry/trainer repeats cannot pay twice.
- All seven coaches preserve the fixed CEFR course. Trainer-completion/skill badges below apply only to explicit specialist practice; coach choice cannot become a course prerequisite.
- Awards do not create gold bonuses, locked coaches, paid AI requirements, levels or new available content. Private progress, opt-in evidence retention and the existing no-leaderboard/no-auto-share policy remain unchanged.

## States and assessment availability

Track not yet earned, earned, not yet available, insufficient evidence and supporting evidence unavailable after deletion. Eligibility and presentation are separate: a badge can be earned without playing animation, and disabled decoration cannot change rewards. Keep historical completion distinct from currently supported skill; final deletion/invalidation/currency-reversal policy is unresolved and cannot justify resurrecting private media or automatic debt.

Do not add rewards for installing the app, choosing a mascot, adding a provider key, spending money/gold, consuming AI calls, sharing privately held work, watching an unverified external link or merely opening Progress. This adopted catalog recognizes learning activity/evidence rather than those setup/consumption events.
