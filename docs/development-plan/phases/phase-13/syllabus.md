# Phase 13 topic syllabus

137 current topic lessons. The 296 source lessons are retained as exact-version archives. New topics do not inherit earlier completion.

## business

**Foundation**

- Everyday work talk: Introductions and meetings; Emails and status updates
- Interviews and feedback: Structured answers: PREP and STAR; Plain language and feedback

**Intermediate**

- Lead and persuade at work: Meetings and polite pushback; Data and escalation
- Connect and follow up: Follow-up and networking; Calls and receiving feedback

**Advanced**

- Lead with influence: Stakeholders and executive summaries; Requests and difficult conversations
- Work across contexts: Working across contexts; Crisis communication and tough questions

## copywriter

**Foundation**

- Know your message: Benefits and the central message; Headlines and truthful claims
- Write the copy: AIDA and calls to action; Tone and editing

**Intermediate**

- Persuade with proof: Reader research and proof; Objections and stories
- Write for each channel: Emails and landing pages; Testing and honest urgency

**Advanced**

- Message strategy: Positioning and brand voice; Long copy and ethical persuasion
- Craft and responsibility: Taglines across cultures; Claims, disclosure and the brief

## creative-writer

**Foundation**

- People and places: Character and setting; Sensory detail: show and tell
- Scenes and plot: Dialogue and point of view; Plot, beginnings and endings

**Intermediate**

- Sharpen the craft: Voice and specific detail; Figurative language and conflict
- Shape and revise: Subtext and pacing; Revision and feedback

**Advanced**

- Master the craft: Narrative distance and structure; Theme, motif and unreliable narration
- Finish a project: Rhythm and research; Finishing and sharing a project

## debater

**Foundation**

- Build an argument: Claims, reasons and evidence; Argument structure and language
- Answer and test arguments: Rebuttal and fair representation; Logical traps and weighing arguments

**Intermediate**

- Use evidence well: Sources and numbers; Causation and analogy
- Structure a case: Case structure and definitions; Questions, concessions and refutation

**Advanced**

- Argue with rigor: Warrants and burden of proof; Charity and the crux
- Win the exchange: Framing and layered rebuttal; Uncertainty and closing arguments

## grammarian

**Starter**

- Parts of speech: Nouns; Pronouns; Articles and determiners; Adjectives and comparisons; Adverbs; Conjunctions and interjections
- Build sentences: Sentence building
- Be, have and do: Be, have and do

**Foundation**

- Useful forms: Prepositions; Questions
- More about words: Verbs
- Sentence building blocks: Phrases, clauses and sentence structures; Agreement and complete sentences

**Intermediate**

- Time and tense: Modals; Conditionals and wishes
- Build complex sentences: Relative and reduced clauses; Reported speech; Linking ideas and cohesion
- The twelve tenses: Tenses
- Punctuation: Punctuation

**Advanced**

- Register and style: Formal language and noun phrases
- Voice and mood: Active, passive and causative voice; Mood and emphasis
- Common errors: Confusable words; Modifiers and parallel structure

## journalist

**Foundation**

- Ask good questions: News questions: the 5 Ws and H; Follow-ups, quotations and attribution
- Write the story: News structure, ledes and headlines; Facts, opinions and fair summaries

**Intermediate**

- Report accurately: Verification and source agreements; Numbers and fair representation
- Craft the story: Ledes, quotations and paraphrases; Clear sentences and corrections

**Advanced**

- Investigate and verify: Documents and data; Verification and accountability
- Depth and ethics: Features and explanations; Privacy, ethics and fair comment

## negotiator

**Foundation**

- Persuade: Persuasive appeals and audience; Pitches and ethical persuasion
- Negotiate: Interests and offers; Concessions and polite refusals

**Intermediate**

- Bargain and trade: Preparation and first offers; Trading and listening
- Handle tense moments: Objections and boundaries; De-escalation and confirmation

**Advanced**

- Negotiate strategically: Criteria and alternatives; Packages and counteroffers
- Complex and tense deals: Culture and pressure tactics; Authority and renegotiation

## public-speaker

**Foundation**

- Structure and words: Openings, closings and signposts; Rhythm, contrast and questions
- Delivery: Pause, stress and vocal variety; Body language and nerves

**Intermediate**

- Craft the talk: Audience, purpose and structure; Stories and language for the ear
- Present with confidence: Movement and slides; Questions and recovery

**Advanced**

- Rhetorical craft: Credibility and rhetorical figures; Metaphor and speech structures
- Command the room: Adapting to difficult audiences; Ceremonies and online speaking

## reading-listening

**Starter**

- Gist and detail: Gist and supporting details; Scanning and listening for key words
- Main idea and meaning: Main ideas and context clues; Signals, facts and opinions

**Foundation**

- Read longer texts: Skimming and paragraph structure; Reference and sequence
- Listen in real time: Numbers and spoken instructions; Speakers, purpose and notes

**Intermediate**

- Read between the lines: Inference, purpose and tone; Arguments and summaries
- Listen for meaning: Attitude and lecture notes; Comparing sources and bias

**Advanced**

- Analyze texts critically: Implication and rhetorical moves; Evidence and synthesis
- Follow complex speech: Long talks and reduced speech; Debates and shifts in register

## storyteller

**Foundation**

- Shape the story: The moment and the story arc; Detail, hooks and endings
- Tell it aloud: Voice and believable people; Audience awareness and rehearsal

**Intermediate**

- Build the story: Causality and character choices; Details, setup and payoff
- Tell it with presence: Presence and story length; Callbacks and deliberate practice

**Advanced**

- The art of the told story: Layers, restraint and implication; True stories and physical presence
- Tell it in the real world: Live audiences and traditional tales; Workplace stories and ownership
