# Coach syllabus audit — 2026-10-26 (Claude)

**The Grammarian was the real problem, and Phase 11 fixed it.** It went from 32 lessons to 72, with parts of speech, sentence structure, all twelve tenses, punctuation, mood and common errors.

The other nine coaches have 24 lessons each (3 stages × 2 units × 4 lessons). Their topic choice is sound for a self-study app, but each one has a few clear gaps. Suggested additions are listed below, biggest gap first. Each item would become one lesson of 12 auto-checked activities.

| Coach | Covered well | Biggest gaps (suggested lessons) |
|---|---|---|
| **Public Speaking & Rhetoric** | Structure, delivery, rhetoric, slides, Q&A, difficult rooms | **Impromptu speaking** (PREP and other quick structures; one-minute answers), speech types (informative, persuasive, special occasion), vocal warm-up and articulation, using humour |
| **Creative Writing** | Character, scene, voice, revision, narrative distance | **Poetry basics** (image, line, sound, rhythm and simple forms), flash fiction, genre conventions, writing description vs exposition |
| **Debater & Argument** | Claims, evidence, fallacies (four), rebuttal, burden of proof, crux | More fallacies (appeal to authority or popularity, hasty generalisation, circular reasoning, red herring), debate formats and speaker roles, timing a speech, the Toulmin model by name |
| **Reading & Listening** | Gist, detail, inference, tone, bias, synthesis, fast speech | Reading charts and data, text structures (compare/contrast, cause/effect, problem/solution), connected speech (linking, weak forms) for listening |
| **Journalist** | 5 Ws, pyramid, ledes, sourcing, verification, ethics | News values (what makes news), op-eds and editorials, writing for audio and video, interview technique in depth |
| **Business & Professional** | Meetings, email, interviews, feedback, stakeholders, crisis | Reports and minutes, numbers and money language (percentages, trends), handling complaints and apologising, phone calls and voicemail |
| **Advertising & Copywriting** | Benefits, headlines, AIDA, proof, channels, ethics | Social media posts, product descriptions, UX microcopy (buttons, errors), writing for search |
| **Persuasion & Negotiation** | Interests, offers, trading, objections, alternatives, culture | Anchoring and the zone of agreement by name, negotiating by email, influence principles in negotiation |
| **Storytelling** | Moment, change, telling aloud, callbacks, ethics | Classic story shapes (the hero's journey, rags to riches), the short "about me" story, humour in stories |

## Recommendation

1. **Public Speaking:** add *Impromptu speaking*. It matches your goal of speaking better, and it pairs well with Talk in the Lab.
2. **Creative Writing:** add *Poetry basics*. It is the only missing area that a learner would call missing.
3. **Debater:** add *More fallacies*. This is quick to add and useful.

Each of these is about 4 lessons, added as a new unit with `track_lib.add_units` in the same way as the Grammarian. Earlier lessons and saved progress stay untouched. Tell me which ones to build, if any.
